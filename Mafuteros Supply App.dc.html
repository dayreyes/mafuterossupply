Español or English chosen at the door, Zelle / Venmo with a 30‑minute hold on cash, and one delivery run a day that you control. Tap through it — everything works.

The rules I built in
  • Pay by app and it’s separado — the jar is yours until you show.
  • Cash pickup gets 30 minutes, then the order releases and the strain goes back on the menu.
  • Cash on delivery is fine — he’s the one coming to you.
  • One run a day from Temple Terrace. Free inside 5 miles, $5 out to 10, then $1 more every 5 miles.
  • You set the window, the stop count, the cutoff zone, or kill delivery for the day.
